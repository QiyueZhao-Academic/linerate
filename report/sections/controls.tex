\section{Controls}
\label{sec:controls}

Each control carries a prediction made before the measurement and recorded next
to the outcome. A control whose prediction is written down afterwards cannot
fail, and a control that cannot fail is decoration. Of \ctlRan\ controls that
ran, \ctlPassed\ passed. Table~\ref{tab:controls} gives each one with its
prediction and what happened.

\noindent\textbf{The null cipher.}
Replacing the AEAD with a copy of the same length leaves everything else in the
hot path unchanged: the same system calls, the same replay window, the same
header parse, the same buffers. What remains is the fixed cost of the data plane
with the cryptography removed, \ctlNullFixed\ cycles against \ctlAEADFixed\ with
it. The difference, \ctlAEADOnlyFixed\ cycles, is what the AEAD contributes to
$a$. It is per-message setup and teardown, it does not scale with payload, and
it therefore cannot be amortised by sending larger packets. Without this
control, $a$ would be a single opaque number; with it, it decomposes.

Whether the per-byte terms of the two configurations can be compared at all
depends on the host. Where the fixed cost dwarfs the per-byte term across the
whole payload range, two small slopes differ by less than the fit resolves, and
requiring one to fall below the other would make this control fail on noise. The
control measures that condition rather than assuming it. On this host the
per-byte terms are separately identifiable: \ctlPerByteIdentifiable.

\noindent\textbf{The masking control.}
If clearing the hardware crypto capability bits did not raise $b$ by a large
factor, the mask never reached the implementation, and every masked measurement
in Section~\ref{sec:costmodel} would be void. This is the control that makes the
masked arm trustworthy rather than merely present.

\noindent\textbf{Instrumentation controls.}
Three checks confirm that the harness is not measuring itself. The cost of
polling an empty socket is weighted by how often that actually happens in a run,
because a poll that costs a tenth of a packet but occurs once per thousand
packets contaminates nothing; the amortised figure is the one with a threshold
attached. A forged packet is required to cost about what a valid one costs, so
that rejection is neither a cheap path through the loop nor a side channel. And
every cipher level in the design is confirmed constructible, since a missing
level would silently shrink the experiment.
